## ✨ 新功能

- **单次占卜的 AI 总预算**：新增 `ai.ai_budget_seconds`（默认 120 秒）——一次占卜里所有 AI 调用（「牌灵的话」与深度解读，含换个模型重试）共用一个截止点，预算见底就回退本地牌义，不再往下发请求；设为 0 可关掉这条保护，此时 AI 阶段的等待时长没有上界，日志里会留一条提示。
- **解读结构校验强度可选**：新增 `ai.structure_check`（默认 `shadow` 影子档）——解读照旧原样发给用户，但另外按严格规则判一遍、记一行日志，用来量化真实候选里到底有多少会被严格规则拒掉，以及**按严格口径这一签最终会不会整段降级**——后者是三态判决：不降级（已有候选严格通过）／降级（整条可用候选链已观测完且无候选严格通过）／未观测（宽松口径已放行收尾，严格口径会继续试的那些候选本次没跑，就只报未知），并附实例级累计读数（单签样本不足凭，看的是长期分布）；确认误拒率可接受后可在面板切 `strict`，那时结构不合格的解读整体回退本地牌义，而不是把半截编号发出去。三档：`loose` 宽松（旧行为，只要求分段数不少于牌数）、`shadow` 影子（默认，不改任何行为）、`strict` 严格（编号须 1..N 各一次、【总结】0 或 1 次、位置名不锁死但不得为空）。

## 🔧 修复

- **AI 卡住时不再无限等下去**：某个模型长时间不响应、或几个模型接连失败时，一次占卜的 AI 部分从前能拖得比单个请求超时长得多（每换一个模型重试就重新计时）；现在它有上界——连「挑一个模型」这一步的异步取用也算在这笔预算里，牌灵的话与深度解读共用一个截止点，超了就地回退（解读回退内置牌义、牌灵的话回退当日签文池），牌面与牌义照常送到。
- **AI 文案不再抢走整卦的时间**：牌灵的话属于次要内容，另受 20 秒的阶段上限约束，不会独吞整笔预算，轮到深度解读时还有时间可用。这条上限是**整个阶段共享**的：连「取候选」那一步异步取用也算在内，多个候选接连失败也不会按「20 秒 × 候选数」往后累加（旧实现每个候选各给一次 20 秒，两个候选就能等到 40 秒）；阶段用尽即收手，剩余预算留给深度解读。关掉总预算（`ai_budget_seconds=0`）时，这条阶段上限照样生效。
- **热重载不再擦掉正要发的图**：每个插件实例拥有自己的图片目录，启动时只回收**过期**（三天没写过新东西）的旧实例残骸，不再清扫整个共享目录。免删范围按 PID 判定：**同一进程的任何实例目录一律不碰**——热重载的前后两代就是同 PID，旧实例的图可能还在等发送，只跳过「自己那一个目录」是不够的。实例目录建不出来时改用进程私有的临时目录，绝不退回共享目录；连临时目录都建不出来就降级为无图，不影响文字结果送达。
- **影子日志给出拒绝率百分比**：影子汇总的本次与累计两处都写明「分子/分母 (百分比)」，一眼能读出候选拒绝率；分母为零时写 `N/A` 而不是 `0%`（0/0 是「还没观测量」，不是「没被拒」）。累计值只属于当前解释器实例（热重载或重启即清零），日志里已经写明这一点：观察期的总体拒绝率要用各次观测的「Σ拒绝 ÷ Σ候选」，别平均各行百分比、也别跨代累加。
- **「今天」只有一种算法**：今日牌运、牌灵的话、每日计数共用同一个日期来源，时区优先跟随框架全局配置里的 `timezone`（为空或名字非法才退回系统本地时区），不再各算各的。跨时区部署、或服务器时区与使用习惯不一致时，不会再出现「刚过零点的人拿到昨天的牌」。
- **解读记事本不再无限增长**：同一天按主题缓存的解读最多保留 20 条，超出后**最早问过的那个主题**让位——之后再问它，会重新生成一段（内容可能与原来不一样）。这条只管结构、不改判据：泛问照旧共用当日那一段，「今天财运怎么样」与「今天财运如何」照旧是两个桶。
- **命令入口的收尾句不再抢在结果前面**：合并转发卡的到达可能比紧随其后的纯文本慢，收尾句会先到、卡片后到；现在收尾句等一个短窗口再发，结果先到、收尾句后到。结果本体不受影响，照旧先发先到。纯文字 / 纯图模式没有这层滞后，本来就即发即到，不多等那一下。


- **`ai_timeout=0` = 不限制单个请求**：单次 AI 解读超时写 `0`，从前会被静默抬到 5 秒，现在就是「不限制」——此时只有「单次占卜的 AI 总预算」（`ai_budget_seconds`）与牌灵的话的阶段上限还管着它，两个上界都还在，最坏耗时照旧有界。其余取值照旧不低于 5 秒——1~4 秒与负数都按 5 秒算：「请求还没发出去就注定超时」不是自由度，是自残；写 `0` 才是关掉这一档。
- **自然语言入口的 AI 失败时不再一句解释都没有**：命令入口一直有「AI 今天闹脾气不肯开口，牌面先给你」这样一句说明，自然语言入口（在对话里直接说「帮我算一卦」）此前没有——AI 全挂、解读退回内置牌义时，用户看到的是一段安静降级的文字。现在两个入口都会说明这次是回退结果。
- **依赖安全下限提升**：Pillow 运行下限从 `>=10.0.0` 提升到 `>=12.3.0`。12.3.0 是 OSV 现查无已知条目的最低版本；10.x / 11.x 仍带已知 CVE。安装/升级时会要求更新的 Pillow，渲染逻辑不变。

**English**

## ✨ New features

- **A per-reading AI time budget**: `ai.ai_budget_seconds` (default 120) — every AI call in one reading (the spirit's line and the in-depth reading, retries on another model included) shares a single deadline; once the budget is spent it falls back to the built-in meanings instead of starting another request. Set it to 0 to turn the guard off: the AI stage then has no upper bound, and the log says so.
- **Configurable strictness for the structure check**: a new `ai.structure_check` option (default `shadow`) — readings still go out exactly as before, but every candidate is additionally judged by the strict rules and one line is logged, so you can measure how many real candidates those rules would reject — and whether, under the strict rules, this very reading would have degraded to the built-in meanings. That verdict has three states: no degradation (a candidate passed); degradation (the whole usable candidate chain was observed and none passed); or *unobserved* (the loose pass ended the chain early, so the candidates the strict rules would have tried next never ran — reported as unknown instead of a guess). A running total comes with it, since one reading proves nothing. Once the false-rejection rate looks acceptable, switch to `strict` in the plugin panel and a structurally invalid reading falls back to the built-in meanings wholesale instead of shipping a half-numbered text. Three levels: `loose` (the old behaviour — section count no less than the number of cards), `shadow` (default, changes nothing), `strict` (numbers 1..N exactly once,【总结】zero or one time, position names free-form but non-empty).

## 🔧 Fixes

- **A stuck AI no longer keeps the reading waiting**: when a provider hangs, or several fail one after another, the AI part of a reading could take far longer than a single request timeout, since every retry restarted its own clock. It now has a ceiling — even the asynchronous "pick a model" step draws on the same budget, the spirit's line and the in-depth reading share one deadline, and when that runs out the plugin falls back on the spot (built-in meanings for the reading, the day's pool for the spirit's line), while the cards and their meanings still arrive.
- **The spirit's line no longer eats the whole wait**: as the secondary piece it has its own 20-second stage cap, so it cannot spend the entire budget and leave the in-depth reading with nothing. That cap is shared across the whole stage — the asynchronous "pick a model" step counts against it too, and several failing candidates no longer add up to 20 seconds each (the old code handed every candidate its own 20 seconds, so two candidates could wait 40). The moment the stage runs out it stops, and whatever is left goes to the in-depth reading. Turning the total budget off (`ai_budget_seconds=0`) still leaves this stage cap in force.
- **A hot reload no longer wipes the image it was about to send**: every plugin instance gets its own image directory, and startup only reclaims **stale** leftovers (nothing written for three days) instead of sweeping a shared directory. The exemption is decided by PID: **no instance directory owned by the running process is touched at all** — the two generations either side of a hot reload share that PID, and the older one's image may still be waiting to be sent, so skipping just "my own directory" is not enough. When the instance directory cannot be created the plugin falls back to a process-private temporary directory, never to a shared one; if even that fails the reading simply goes out without an image.
- **The shadow log now reports a rejection rate**: both the per-reading and the running-total figures are printed as `numerator/denominator (percentage)`, so the rate reads straight off the line. A zero denominator prints `N/A`, not `0%` (0/0 means nothing observed yet, not nothing rejected). The running total belongs to the current interpreter instance only — a hot reload or restart clears it — and the log says so: compute an observation window's overall rate from the summed rejections over the summed candidates, never by averaging per-line percentages or adding up successive generations.
- **There is only one way to compute "today"**: the daily card, the spirit's line and the daily counters all share a single date source, and its timezone follows the framework's global `timezone` setting first (falling back to the system local timezone only when it is empty or unparsable). Cross-timezone deployments — or a host clock that disagrees with local custom — no longer hand yesterday's card to someone who just passed midnight.
- **The day's reading notebook no longer grows without bound**: readings cached per topic for a single day are capped at 20, and past that the earliest topic makes room — ask it again and it is generated afresh, so the wording may differ from before. This is structure only, not a change of criteria: generic questions still share the day's one reading, and two differently worded money questions remain two separate entries.
- **The closing line no longer jumps ahead of the result on the command path**: a forwarded card can take longer to arrive than a plain message sent right after it, so the closing line used to land first and the card afterwards; it now waits a short moment, which puts the result first and the closing line after it. The result itself is unaffected and goes out as before. Text-only and image-only modes have no such lag, so they send straight away without an extra wait.
- **`ai_timeout=0` means "no cap on a single request"**: setting the per-request interpretation timeout to `0` used to be silently raised to 5s; now it leaves that stage uncapped. The total AI budget (`ai_budget_seconds`) and the spirit-line stage cap still bound the wait, so the worst case stays finite. Any other value is still floored at 5 seconds (1-4 and negatives all become 5): a timeout that expires before the request goes out is not freedom, it is self-harm; only an explicit `0` turns the cap off.
- **The natural-language entry now explains an AI failure instead of staying silent**: the command entry has always added a line such as "the AI is in a mood today, here are the cards anyway"; the natural-language entry (just say "read my cards" in chat) had none, so when every provider failed and the reading fell back to the built-in meanings the user saw a quietly downgraded text. Both entries now say so.
- **Dependency security floor raised**: the Pillow runtime floor is now `>=12.3.0`, the lowest version with no OSV advisories at the time of writing; 10.x / 11.x still carry known CVEs. Installation/upgrade will require the newer Pillow; rendering behaviour is unchanged.
