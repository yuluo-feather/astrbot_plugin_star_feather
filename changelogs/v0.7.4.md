## 🔧 修复

- **「最近其他事情怎么样」「今天弹吉他怎么样」这类问法，不再被当成有具体主题**：从前句子里只要带一个「他」，就被看作是在为某件具体事发问——「其他」「他日」「吉他」这些词里正好都有它，于是这类问法错过当天的固定牌、改成每问一次重新抽；现在它们按泛问处理，跟「最近怎么样」一样，给你当天那一张。

**English**

- **Questions that merely contain the character 他 are no longer read as being about one specific matter**: plenty of ordinary Chinese words happen to contain it, so asking how things are otherwise — or how a guitar session went — used to miss the card of the day and get a fresh draw instead; those questions now count as general fortune questions and hand you today's card the same way a plain how-have-you-been does.
