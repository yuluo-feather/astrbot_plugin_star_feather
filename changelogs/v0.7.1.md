## 🔧 修复

- **AI 报错不再吞掉整卦**：AI 服务出错（超时、限流、接口报错）时，线上会返回「这卦起得有点乱……换个时候再来问」——一次 AI 文案失败，连带牌面、牌义、签文全都拿不到；现在丢的只是那两句 AI 文案，解读回退本地牌义、「牌灵的话」回退当日池内签文，牌照常算给你
- **牌面图不会再让整卦丢掉**：偶尔会出现整条消息都没收到——牌面图在发出前就已经不在本地了，平台读到空文件，整条解读跟着被拒；现在发送前会核验一次，图不在了就当场补一张，最坏也只是没有图，解读一定到手

**English**

- **AI errors no longer swallow a whole reading**: when the AI service failed (timeout, rate limit, API error) the whole reading collapsed into "this one came out muddled, ask again later", taking the cards, their meanings and the line down with it; now only the two AI-written passages are lost — the reading falls back to the built-in meanings and the spirit's line to the one drawn for that day, and the cards still come through
- **A missing card image no longer costs you the reading**: every so often nothing arrived at all — the card image had already left local storage before the message went out, the platform read an empty file and rejected the whole reply; the image is now checked right before sending and redrawn on the spot if it is gone, so the worst case is a reading without a picture instead of no reading at all
