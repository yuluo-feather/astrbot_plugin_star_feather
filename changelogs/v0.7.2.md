## 🔧 修复

- **起卦正好撞上重载或进程收尾时，不再整条丢掉**：那一刻牌面图已经画好、解读也写好了，却可能整条都收不到——收尾那一环没走通，把整卦一起拖了下去；现在这一步失败只影响它自己，卦照常送到你手上
- **一长串「说说说说…」这样的开头不再拖住回复**：从前这类文本会让消息迟迟没有回音——越长越糟，几十个字就够把这一条卡住；现在它按普通消息处理，该占卜的照常占卜，不会再无声无息地拖下去

**English**

- **A reading that lands right on a reload or shutdown is no longer lost whole**: at that moment the card image had been drawn and the text written, yet the whole reading could fail to arrive — a closing step that did not go through dragged everything down with it; that step now absorbs its own failure, and the reading reaches you as usual
- **Text starting with a long run of 「说说说说…」 no longer holds up the reply**: it used to leave your message unanswered for a long time — the longer the run, the worse it got, and a few dozen characters were enough to stall it; such text is now handled like any other message and you get your reading as usual
