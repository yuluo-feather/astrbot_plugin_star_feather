## ✨ 新增

- **观测台背景改为动态水面**：星空以地平线为轴实时镜像成倒影，倒影带轻微错位和水面波纹；倒影里的星点会随波纹晃动；星云按原场景 `hue_shift` 的 `speed=0.08` 做全色谱循环（约 12.5 秒一圈），地平线保留白色高光带
- **插件观测台（WebUI 可视化视图）**：在 AstrBot 插件详情页打开「星羽塔罗 · 观测台」，只读查看今日牌运记录、各主题解读、78 张牌面预览与牌意图鉴（关键词、正逆牌义），另有四种牌阵的牌位与适用场景说明；支持正逆位切换与大图查看；背景为原画整图（水面换成星空镜像合成，已去掉人物倒影与波纹），星点与流星复用原场景的星芒贴图，星云缓慢漂移（致谢见 README）。附「占卜预览」工作台：选牌阵、填问题、本地抽牌看牌义，AI 深度解读只在点击后请求，不消耗次数、不写缓存、不发送消息
- **观测台恢复近景流星并隔离背景色相**：Canvas 近景流星重新启用，固定青碧色、约 30° 右下方向、连续三层尾迹（柔光、主体、亮芯），同时最多 2 颗；背景与水面继续 12.5 秒色相循环，但色相容器只包环境层，流星和界面不再随背景偏色
- **远景流星拆成 A/B 两类**：A 类更短更暗、约占三分之二，构成背景；B 类更长更亮、长度差异大，形成纵深；角度在 28°–36° 之间轻微变化，金色余晖与流星同向，出现频率保持 0.8–1.9 秒
- **水面波纹减弱**：倒影湍流位移由 17 收到 11，频率上限由 0.0044 0.021 降到 0.0036 0.016，水面保留轻微扰动但不再像被搅动；天空与水面色相继续同步循环
- **近景流星加入星芒呼吸与独立流星群**：星芒按 1.4–1.7 秒独立呼吸（尺寸略滞后），尾迹与运动按青碧色基线细调；原有单颗近景流星保持 6–12 秒一颗、上限 2 颗，并新增一套独立计时的流星群层——每 7–10 秒一组 2–3 颗、组内间隔 180–320ms、同组同向，新增顶部与左侧入口，群组独立上限 3 颗
- **近景流星加入局部动态色相染色**：在背景星云所在区域定义一片固定色相区域，近景流星的头部柔光与尾迹按采样点位置取色；区域外保持青碧色，进入区域后随背景 12.5 秒色相周期变化，离开立即恢复；白色亮芯与十字星芒不参与染色，水面倒影复用天空颜色。远景 CSS 流星与背景色相保持不变

## 🔧 修复

- **观测台日索引在满员后正确轮换**：同一进程里多个实例共用一份 KV 时，某个实例淘汰的用户不会因为另一个实例的本地记账而被永久跳过；被淘汰用户再次活跃时能重新进入今日名单
- **今日牌运列表优先展示最近活跃的用户**：索引超过 200 位时显示最近登记的有效记录，列表面板提示索引覆盖人数；缺当天记录的条目不再占用展示名额
- **更新后不再返回旧版本生成的今日解读**：提示词更新后的当天，先前生成的解读会被判定为过期并重新生成，不会再顶着新版本继续使用
- **关闭 AI 后不再返回历史 AI 内容**：`ai.enable_ai=false` 时，今日牌运的深度解读回退内置牌义，牌灵的话回退签文池；此前当天已生成的 AI 内容会被直接返回
- **严格结构校验覆盖已缓存的解读**：`ai.structure_check=strict` 时，宽松口径下留下的不合规解读会被视为未命中并重新生成，不必等到缓存过期
- **配置里的负数不再静默关闭保护**：`ai_fail_cooldown`、`llm_tool_cooldown`、`cmd_rate_limit` 与 `daily_count` 的负数取值按非法值回退默认；只有显式 0 才表示关闭
- **观测台列表按最近登记排序**：今日牌运列表不再按用户 ID 重排，打开页面时默认选中最近登记的那位
- **占卜预览拒绝非布尔的正逆位**：接口收到 `"false"`、`null` 等非布尔值时返回 400，不再把逆位误判成正位
- **观测台交互反馈更清晰**：按钮、标签页、用户列表与牌面卡片在按下时有轻微压缩反馈；键盘焦点统一为金色描边；切换标签页时新面板轻微淡入。开启系统「减少动态效果」后自动关闭位移与缩放动画
- **占卜预览按张揭示**：抽牌后三张或四张牌以 3D 翻转依次揭示（单张 450ms、间隔 130ms），所有正面就绪后统一起跑；牌背取自已内置素材；缩略图加载前显示占位；AI 解读完成时只更新解读区域，不再重播牌面
- **近景流星接近地平线时逐点淡出**：尾迹按采样点各自计算透明度，靠近地平线的点先淡出，仍在天上的部分保持亮度；头部到地平线时再移除，不再整颗一起变淡或突兀消失
- **占卜预览刷新会清空已生成结果**：在预览页点顶部「刷新」会清空当前预览与 AI 解读，回到空状态；表单保留，可重新抽牌
- **观测台今日详情只返回当天记录**：详情接口与今日列表口径一致，昨天的记录直接请求也返回 404，不再露出旧详情
- **快速切换用户时不再显示旧详情**：同一用户连续刷新时，先发出、后返回的旧请求（无论成功还是失败）不会覆盖最新详情
- **预览解读拒绝重复牌**：同一张牌不能重复出现在多个牌位，与正式抽牌的无放回规则一致
- **观测台概览改为固定并发读取**：今日概览统计对成员记录最多 8 路并发读取，索引接近 500 时一次刷新不再逐个串行等待；统计口径与返回结构不变
- **缩略图重复请求合并**：同一张牌、同一尺寸的并发取图只发一次请求；失败后清理在途状态，刷新可重试；不同尺寸与正逆位仍各自独立

## ✨ New

- **The observer background now has a live water surface**: the sky is mirrored about the horizon in real time with a slight offset and an animated ripple; reflected stars move with the water; the nebula cycles through the full hue spectrum at the original scene's `hue_shift` speed of 0.08 (about one cycle every 12.5 s), with the white horizon highlight preserved
- **Observer view (WebUI plugin page)**: open "Star Feather · Observer" from the plugin detail page to browse today's daily readings, topic interpretations, all 78 cards with keywords and upright/reversed meanings, and the four spreads with position notes and use cases, in a read-only view; includes a reversed-card toggle and lightbox, set against the full original artwork (the water is a mirrored-sky composite, so no character reflections or ripples remain), with star sprites and meteors reusing the scene's own flare texture and a slowly drifting nebula (credits in the README). Also includes a divination preview workbench: pick a spread, ask a question, draw locally and read the built-in meanings; AI interpretation runs only on click and uses no quota, writes no cache and sends no messages
- **The observer's near meteors are back, with the backdrop hue isolated**: the Canvas near meteors are enabled again with a fixed cyan-teal color, a ~30° down-right heading, a continuous three-layer trail (halo, body, bright core), and at most two at once; the sky and water still cycle through the full hue spectrum every 12.5 s, but the hue container now holds only the environment layers, so meteors and UI no longer shift color with the background
- **Distant meteors split into A/B variants**: the A variant is shorter and dimmer (about two thirds of spawns) and forms the background layer; the B variant is longer, brighter and more varied in length, adding depth; angles vary between 28° and 36°, golden embers follow the same heading, and the spawn rate stays at 0.8–1.9 s
- **Calmer water ripple**: the reflection displacement drops from 17 to 11 and the frequency peak from `0.0044 0.021` to `0.0036 0.016`, keeping a light ripple without the churned look; sky and water hue cycles stay in sync
- **Near meteors now breathe and gain an independent group layer**: each head flares on its own 1.4–1.7 s cycle with the size lagging slightly behind opacity, and the trail and motion follow the refined cyan-teal baseline; the original solo near meteor keeps its 6–12 s cadence with a cap of two, while a separately timed group layer appears every 7–10 s with two or three meteors 180–320 ms apart on a shared heading, entering from the top or the left, with its own cap of three
- **Near meteors now use a local hue field**: a fixed hue region follows the background nebula, and the head glow and trail take their color from each sample's position; they stay cyan-teal outside the region, shift with the background's 12.5 s hue cycle inside, and return immediately on exit. The white core and cross flare stay uncolored, and the water reflection reuses the sky colors; distant meteors and the backdrop hue cycle are unchanged

## 🔧 Fixes

- **The observer's daily index now rotates correctly when full**: when several instances share one KV in the same process, a user evicted by one instance is no longer skipped forever because of another instance's local cache; an evicted user who becomes active again re-enters today's list
- **The daily list now surfaces the most recently active users**: when the index holds more than 200 entries the list shows the most recently registered valid records, the panel reports how many users the index covers, and entries without a record for today no longer take up a slot
- **Readings generated before a prompt update are no longer served**: on the same day, interpretations created under an older prompt version are treated as stale and regenerated
- **Turning AI off no longer returns cached AI output**: with `ai.enable_ai=false`, the daily deep reading falls back to the built-in meanings and the spirit line falls back to the pool; previously, AI content generated earlier that day was still returned
- **Strict structure checking now covers cached readings**: with `ai.structure_check=strict`, cached readings that fail the strict rules are treated as a miss and regenerated instead of waiting out the cache
- **Negative config values no longer silently disable protection**: negative values for `ai_fail_cooldown`, `llm_tool_cooldown`, `cmd_rate_limit`, and `daily_count` now fall back to their defaults; only an explicit 0 turns a limit off
- **The observer list now follows registration order**: today's list is no longer re-sorted by user ID, so the most recently registered user is selected by default when the page opens
- **The preview API rejects non-boolean upright flags**: requests carrying `"false"`, `null`, or other non-boolean values now return 400 instead of treating a reversed card as upright
- **Clearer interaction feedback in the observer**: buttons, tabs, user rows, and card tiles now give a slight press response; keyboard focus uses a consistent gold outline; switching tabs fades the incoming panel in. Displacement and scale animations are disabled when the system asks for reduced motion
- **Preview cards reveal one by one**: after a draw, the three or four cards flip over in 3D one after another (450 ms each, 130 ms apart) once all fronts are ready, using the bundled card back; thumbnails show a shimmer placeholder until the front is ready; when the AI reading arrives, only its own block updates instead of replaying the card animation
- **Near meteors now fade out point by point near the horizon**: each trail sample carries its own opacity, so points close to the horizon fade first while the part still in the sky keeps its brightness; the meteor is removed once its head reaches the horizon instead of the whole trail dimming together
- **Refreshing the preview clears generated results**: the header refresh on the preview tab clears the current cards and AI reading, returns to the empty prompt, and keeps the form ready for another draw
- **Today's observer detail only serves today's record**: the detail endpoint now matches the daily list boundary; requesting yesterday's record returns 404 instead of showing stale detail
- **Rapid user switches no longer show stale detail**: when the same user is refreshed twice, an older request that returns later (success or failure) cannot overwrite the newest detail
- **Preview readings reject duplicate cards**: the same card can no longer occupy multiple positions, matching the no-replacement rule of the real draw
- **The observer overview now reads records with bounded concurrency**: today's overview reads member records up to eight at a time, so a refresh no longer waits on nearly 500 serial KV reads; the statistics and response shape are unchanged
- **Duplicate thumbnail requests are merged**: concurrent requests for the same card and size now share one call; a failure clears the in-flight entry so a refresh can retry, and different sizes or orientations stay independent
